Are you a permanent resident of Punjab?

Your CNIC address should show a Punjab district. The scheme is only for residents of Punjab province.

Question 2 of 5

Are you the head of your family as per NADRA record?

Do you own a plot within the size limit?

Complete Information Guide

Everything About Apni Chhat Apna Ghar — In Simple Words

Eligibility, documents, loan details, application steps, and status checking — explained clearly so you can apply confidently on the official portal.

1

What is the Apni Chhat Apna Ghar Program?

Apni Chhat Apna Ghar (اپنی چھت اپنا گھر — "Your Roof, Your Home") is a flagship housing initiative of the Chief Minister of Punjab. Its goal is simple: help low-income families of Punjab who own a small plot but cannot afford construction, so they can finally build a house of their own instead of living on rent or in informal settlements.

Under the program, the Punjab Government provides an interest-free (markup-free) construction loan of up to Rs. 15 lakh (PKR 1.5 million). The program targets 100,000 houses/apartments across the province and is executed by PHATA — the Punjab Housing & Town Planning Agency.

Key point: This is a loan, not a grant — you repay it in easy monthly installments, but you pay zero interest/markup. The government bears the financial cost.

3

Who is Eligible? (Complete Criteria)

You should meet all of these conditions before applying:

  • Punjab resident: Permanent resident of Punjab, verified through your CNIC address.
  • Head of family: You must be the head of your family according to NADRA records — one application per household.
  • Plot ownership: You (the household) must own a plot — up to 5 Marla in urban areas or up to 10 Marla in rural areas — with valid ownership proof (Fard, registry, or allotment letter).
  • NSER / PMT score: Your household should be registered in the National Socio-Economic Registry (NSER) with a PMT score of 60 or less.
  • No loan default: You must not be a defaulter of any bank or financial institution.
  • Clean record: No criminal record.
Not sure about your PMT score? The PMT score comes from the BISP/NSER household survey. If your household was never surveyed, visit your nearest BISP registration center to get surveyed first.
4

Documents Required

Prepare these documents before you start the online application — it makes the process much faster:

  • CNIC / NICOP — clear scan or photo of front and back.
  • Proof of land ownership — Fard (land record), registry, or allotment letter. Land records are verified through PLRA (Punjab Land Records Authority).
  • Possession proof — document or evidence showing you physically possess the plot.
  • Recent photographs — passport-size photos with plain background.
  • Income information — where asked (e.g. salary slip or bank statement), to assess repayment capacity.
  • Affidavit / declaration — as required by the portal, e.g. declaring you own no other house.

All documents are uploaded/verified through the official process. Never hand your original documents to any agent or "facilitator".

5

How to Apply Online — Step by Step

1
Open the official portal

Go to acag.punjab.gov.pk — type it yourself in the browser; don't follow links from SMS or WhatsApp forwards.

2
Register with your CNIC

Enter your CNIC number and mobile number (the SIM should be registered on your own CNIC) and verify via the code you receive.

3
Fill the application form

Provide family, plot, and income details exactly as per your documents. Wrong details are the most common reason for rejection.

4
Attach documents & submit

Upload CNIC, land proof, and photos where required, review everything once, then submit. Save/download your acknowledgement slip.

5
Verification & approval

PHATA and related departments verify your record (NADRA, NSER/PMT, PLRA land record, bank default check). Keep checking your dashboard and SMS for updates.

Applications are rolling: registrations are processed in batches as PHATA verifies them, so apply as soon as your documents are ready.
6

How to Check Your Application Status

After applying, you can track your application any time:

  1. Open acag.punjab.gov.pk and log in with your CNIC number.
  2. Your dashboard shows the current stage — submitted, under verification, approved, or objection.
  3. From the dashboard you can also download your acknowledgement slip and see notifications about next steps.
  4. Keep your registered mobile active — official updates also come via SMS.

If an objection appears (e.g. land record mismatch), read it carefully, fix the document issue, and respond through the portal within the given time.

7

Loan Release, Installments & Repayment

The loan is not given as one lump sum. It is released in stages tied to construction progress, verified physically:

  • Stage 1: initial tranche for foundation/plinth work.
  • Stage 2: released after verification of structure progress (walls/roof).
  • Stage 3: final tranche for finishing work.

Repayment starts after a 3-month grace period and runs for about 7 years in easy monthly installments. Because the loan is markup-free, you only return the principal amount. Your exact monthly installment is stated in your loan agreement — pay on time to keep your record clean.

Use the loan only for construction of the declared plot. Misuse can lead to cancellation and recovery of the amount.
8

Urban vs Rural — What's Different?

ConditionUrban AreasRural Areas
Maximum plot sizeUp to 5 MarlaUp to 10 Marla
Land proofRegistry / allotment / FardFard (PLRA record) most common
Loan & termsSame for both — up to Rs. 15 lakh, interest-free, ~7-year repayment

Everything else — eligibility, documents, application process, and repayment — is the same. The only real difference is the plot size limit and the typical form of land record.

9

Common Mistakes That Get Applications Rejected

  • CNIC address outside Punjab — update your CNIC with NADRA first if you actually live in Punjab.
  • Not the family head — the application must be in the name of the NADRA-recorded head of family.
  • Plot not in your name — transfer/inheritance (intiqal) must be completed in the land record before applying.
  • Plot bigger than the limit — over 5 Marla urban / 10 Marla rural doesn't qualify.
  • PMT score above 60 or household not in NSER survey.
  • Wrong mobile number — use a SIM registered on your own CNIC; OTP and updates go there.
  • Mismatched details — name spellings and plot details must exactly match CNIC and Fard.
10

⚠️ Scam Alert — Protect Yourself

The official registration is FREE. Anyone demanding money to "approve", "speed up", or "guarantee" your Apni Chhat Apna Ghar loan is a fraudster.
  • Never pay any agent, "office", or Facebook/TikTok page for registration or approval.
  • Never share your OTP code, bank PIN, or password with anyone claiming to be from the scheme.
  • Fake websites copy the scheme's name — the only official portal is acag.punjab.gov.pk (note the .gov.pk ending).
  • Government staff never ask for fees via Easypaisa/JazzCash for this program.
  • Report fraud attempts to your local police or cybercrime authorities (NCCIA).
11

Program Progress So Far

The program has moved fast since its launch. Public reporting indicates that tens of thousands of families have already received loans, thousands of homes are completed, and tens of thousands more are under construction across Punjab's districts.

Because these numbers change month to month, treat any figure you read (including here) as a snapshot — the latest official statistics are announced by the Punjab Government and PHATA.
