01

PMT Score of 60 or Below

Your Proxy Means Test (PMT) score must be 60 or lower. This score is already in the NSER (National Socio-Economic Registry) database. If you are registered with BISP or the Ehsaas Program, your score is available. You can verify it at the NADRA official portal.

02

Land Size Limit

You must own land registered in your name. In urban areas, the maximum plot size is 5 marla. In rural areas, it is 10 marla. Ownership is verified through PLRA (Punjab Land Record Authority) or patwari records.

03

No Existing Home

You must not already own a constructed home anywhere in Pakistan. NADRA records are used to verify this. If any property is registered in your name, your application will be rejected.

04

Punjab Resident

You must be a permanent resident of Punjab. Your CNIC must show a Punjab address. Temporary residents or people from other provinces are not eligible.

05

Head of Family

Only the head of family can apply, as per NADRA family tree records. However, since the February 2026 update, married sons living separately can also apply as independent applicants.

Important: If you do not meet even one of these conditions, your application may be rejected. Make sure all your information is correct before applying.
