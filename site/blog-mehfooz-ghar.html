Apna Ghar, Mehfooz Ghar — Complete Guide 2026

Punjab's new interest-free loan scheme for repairing unsafe roofs and adding a floor to an existing home — eligibility, loan amounts, documents and how to apply. اپنا گھر، محفوظ گھر — کمزور چھت کی مرمت اور اضافی منزل کی تعمیر کے لیے نیا بلا سود قرض

Launched July 8, 2026 6 min read Independent guide

Why this scheme exists: After a roof collapse in Kahna, on the outskirts of Lahore, drew attention to how many homes in Punjab's older, low-income neighbourhoods have structurally unsafe roofs, Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif approved a new safety-focused loan category — "Apna Ghar, Mehfooz Ghar" ("My Home, Safe Home") — as an extension of the existing Apni Chhat, Apna Ghar programme.

2 The Two Loan Categories

Both are interest-free and repaid in easy monthly installments, same as regular ACAG.

Roof / Structure RepairFor damaged, weak or unsafe roofs
Rs. 500,000Maximum interest-free loan

For families whose house has a cracked, kaccha or structurally weak roof that needs repair or rebuilding to be safe to live in.

Additional FloorFor expanding an existing house
Rs. 1,000,000Maximum interest-free loan

For families who already own a house and need to add another storey/floor to accommodate a growing family.

3 Who Is Eligible

The Chief Minister directed that eligibility criteria and procedures stay identical to regular Apni Chhat Apna Ghar — no extra burden, no new conditions.

Permanent resident of Punjab, verified through your CNIC/NADRA record.

You are the head of family as recorded by NADRA.

You own the house or the plot it sits on, within the standard size limit — up to 5 marla in urban areas, up to 10 marla in rural areas.

Registered in NSER with a PMT score of 60 or below (same threshold as the main scheme).

No bank loan default and no criminal case or FIR registered against you.

If you already meet regular ACAG eligibility, you meet Mehfooz Ghar eligibility too — the difference is only in what the loan is used for (repair/expansion of an existing home, instead of building a new one on an empty plot).

4 Documents You'll Need

Valid CNIC (front and back, clear scan).

A mobile number registered on your own CNIC — OTP and updates are sent there.

Land/house ownership proof — Fard, registry, or allotment letter for the plot the house is on.

Clear photographs of the roof/structure's current condition — this supports your case that repair is genuinely needed.

5 How to Apply

1
Go to the official portal

Applications are processed through the same portal as regular ACAG — acag.punjab.gov.pk. Type the address yourself; don't follow links from SMS or WhatsApp forwards.

2
Register with your CNIC

Create your account with your CNIC number and mobile number, and verify with the OTP you receive.

3
Select the repair/expansion category

Choose the Mehfooz Ghar option that matches your need — roof/structure repair, or an additional floor — and fill in your property details.

4
Upload documents and submit

Attach your CNIC, ownership proof and condition photos, review everything once, then submit. Save your application/tracking number.

5
Track verification

PHATA verifies your NADRA, NSER/PMT and land records before approval — check your dashboard and SMS for updates.

6 Repayment — What to Expect

Like the main ACAG loan, Mehfooz Ghar is interest-free and repaid in easy monthly installments with a grace period before repayment starts. Based on the standard ACAG repayment pattern, widely reported indicative monthly installments are around Rs. 4,700/month for the Rs. 500,000 repair loan and around Rs. 9,300/month for the Rs. 1,000,000 additional-floor loan.

These installment figures are estimates reported alongside the scheme's announcement, not numbers we've verified on an official rate sheet. Your exact monthly installment and tenure will be stated in your own loan agreement once approved — always follow that document over any figure quoted online, including here.
